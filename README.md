## 2. Karta Pracy (str. 5/15) — Końcówki Rzeczowników, Przymiotników i Czasowników

- **`-ANCE / -ENCE`**: *difference, tolerance, elegance, relevance, competence, convenience, performance*
- **`-TION / -SION`**: *decision, information, acquisition, competition, expansion, innovation*
- **`-DOM`**: *freedom, boredom, wisdom, kingdom*

## 3. Konstrukcje Gramatyczne na Kolosa (ze screena i kart pracy 2/15 oraz 3/15)

### A. `I'd rather` vs `prefer` vs `So / Neither` (Karta 2/15, str. 67)

| Konstrukcja | Teraźniejszość / Przyszłość | Przeszłość (*last year, yesterday, when...*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`I'd rather` (ten sam podmiot!)** | **`+ bezokolicznik bez to (V)`**<br>*He'd rather **live** in NY.*<br>*They'd rather **eat** fish tomorrow.* | **`+ have + III forma (have V3)`**<br>*Jenny would rather **have gone** to the Caribbean last year.*<br>*She'd rather **have studied** psychology at college.* |
| **`I'd rather sb` (inna osoba: *you/we/he*)** | **`+ Past Simple (V2 / didn't V)`**<br>*I'd rather you **didn't do** that again.*<br>*Mum would rather I **stayed** home tonight.*<br>*I'd rather you **bought** the meat.* | **`+ Past Perfect (had + V3)`**<br>*She'd rather we **hadn't argued** last night.*<br>*I'd rather we **had taken** the bus when we went into town.* |

- **3 wzory na `prefer`:**
  1. **`prefer + V-ing / rzeczownik + TO + V-ing / rzeczownik`** (zawsze **`to`**, nigdy *than*!):
     - *I **prefer** (eating) ice-cream **to** chocolate.*
     - *I **prefer** seeing films at the cinema **to** watching them on TV.*
  2. **`(would) prefer + TO V + RATHER THAN + V (bez to)`**:
     - *She **would prefer to see** the sights **rather than go** to the tourist shops.*
     - *Sally **would prefer to go** to a restaurant **rather than see** a film.*
  3. **`would rather + V (bez to) + THAN + V (bez to)`**:
     - *He **would rather play** basketball **than study** for his exams.*
- **`So + operator + I` (twierdzenie) vs `Neither / Nor + operator + I` (przeczenie):**
  - *I love going to the cinema.* → **`So do I.`**
  - *I went to see the film yesterday.* → **`So did I.`**
  - *I am a big fan of Brad Pitt.* → **`So am I.`**
  - *I would love to see his latest film.* → **`So would I.`**
  - *I haven't seen Frank recently.* → **`Neither have I.`**
  - *I didn't go to the party last night.* → **`Neither did I.`**
  - *I couldn't hear a thing.* → **`Neither could I.`**
  - *I will never watch another violent film.* → **`Neither will I.`** *(bo `never` to przeczenie!)*

### B. `I wish / If only` (Karta 3/15, str. 47)
1. **Teraźniejszość (`now / right now`):** **`I wish + Past Simple`**
   - *I wish I **had** a car / a pet.*
   - *I wish I **were / was** at home.*
   - *I wish I **didn't have to** study for a test.*
2. **Przeszłość (`yesterday / last year / didn't...`):** **`I wish + Past Perfect (had + V3)`**
   - *I'm sorry I didn't lock the door.* → *I **wish I had locked** the door.*
   - *I feel awful because I shouted at Mark.* → *I **wish I hadn't shouted** at Mark.*
   - *You spilt oil on your jeans.* → *I **wish I hadn't spilt** oil on my jeans.*
3. **Irytacja cudzym zachowaniem:** **`I wish + inna osoba + would + V`**
   - *I wish my neighbours **would stop** playing loud music!* (nigdy *I wish I would*!)

### C. `I should` (teraz) vs `I should have + V3` (przeszłość!) + Modale Unit 4.2
- **`should / shouldn't + V`** = rada na teraz (*You **should prioritize** relevant factors.*)
- **`should have + V3` / `shouldn't have + V3`** = żal lub krytyka błędu z przeszłości („powinien był to zrobić / nie powinien był tego robić”):
  - *He failed to carry out market research. He **should have done** it earlier.*
  - *You **shouldn't have sent** that rude email yesterday.*
- **`mustn't` (NIE WOLNO / zakaz — *It's not allowed*)** ≠ **`don't have to` (NIE MUSISZ / brak konieczności — *It's not necessary*)**
- Przyszłość od *must*: **`will have to`**; przeszłość: **`had to`**.

### D. Conditionale (0, I, II, III + Mixed)
- **0 (stałe prawa):** `If + Present Simple, Present Simple`
- **I (realna przyszłość):** `If + Present Simple, will + V` (*If you **touch** the iron, you **will burn** yourself.*)
- **II (nierealna teraźniejszość / rada):** `If + Past Simple, would + V` (*If I **were** you, I **wouldn't buy** a car.*)
- **III (przeszłość):** `If + Past Perfect (had + V3), would have + V3` (*If they **had known**, they **would have left** earlier.*)
- **Mixed (przeszły warunek → obecny skutek):** `If + Past Perfect [last year], would + V [now]`

### E. Zdania czasowe i wtrącenia: `until`, `before`, `after`, `as soon as (possible)`, `unless`, `by the time`, `as long as`
1. **O przyszłości:** po `until, before, after, as soon as, when, once, by the time, if, unless, as long as` dajemy **Present Simple / Present Perfect** (CAŁKOWITY ZAKAZ `will`!):
   - *I will call you **as soon as I arrive**.*
   - *We won't start **until the manager arrives**.* (po `until` i `unless` czasownik jest **twierdzący**!)
2. **O przeszłości:** czynność wcześniejsza = **Past Perfect (`had + V3`)**, późniejsza = **Past Simple**:
   - *He didn't leave **until he had finished** the report.*
   - ***After she had analysed** the results, she called a meeting.*
   - *The meeting **had already started** **before / by the time** we **arrived**.*
3. **Bez podmiotu po `before` / `after` → forma `-ing`:**
   - ***Before signing** the contract, check the figures.*
4. **Stałe wtrącenia:**
   - **`as soon as possible` (ASAP)** — *tak szybko, jak to możliwe*
   - **`as long as` / `provided that`** — *pod warunkiem, że / dopóki*
   - **`Firstly,` / `To begin with,`** → **`In addition (to this),` / `Furthermore,`** → **`However,`** → **`All things considered,`**

### Unit 4.5: Reasons, Results & Comparison
- **`due to` / `because of` / `as a result of` + RZECZOWNIK / `-ing`** (przyczyna: *Sales fell **due to** poor product quality.*)
- **`because` + PEŁNE ZDANIE** (*Sales fell **because** there was a new competitor.*)
- **`has led to` / `has resulted in` / `resulting in` + RZECZOWNIK / `-ing`** (skutek: *Poor quality **has led to** unhappy customers.*)
- **`in order to` + BEZOKOLICZNIK** (cel: *redesigned **in order to** attract clients.*)
- **`fewer` + POLICZALNE (`fewer stores / fewer customers`)** vs **`less` + NIEPOLICZALNE (`less money / less time`)**
- **`far / much / considerably / slightly` + stopień wyższy** (*far more difficult*, NIGDY *very more*!)
